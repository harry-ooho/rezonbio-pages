Development

What does biosimilar development involve?

Biosimilar development means building a biologic that is highly similar to an approved reference product, with no clinically meaningful differences, and proving it to regulators. Most programs at Rezon Bio move through six stages, targeting market entry in less than 3 years.TBC #5

Read why analytical characterization sits at the center of biosimilar development
  1. 01

    Reference product and target definition

    We source and characterize the reference product to define the target product profile and the critical quality attributes (CQAs) your biosimilar has to match.

  2. 02

    Analytical and functional characterization

    Orthogonal methods for CQAs, including peptide mapping by LC-MS, plus functional and cell-based potency assays that reflect the mode of action, developed early because they are often the most technically challenging.

  3. 03

    Cell line and process development

    Our process development team covers cell line development, with clone selection that weighs product quality against titer, not titer alone. Ambr® 15 and 250 systems support early process development.TBC #7

  4. 04

    Scale-up and technology transfer

    Tech transfers into Rezon Bio or between our two sites, with MS&T and QA involved before transfer, not after. 12 tech transfers completed since operations began.

  5. 05

    GMP manufacturing and PPQ

    Clinical and commercial drug substance in single-use bioreactors up to 2 x 2,000 L. Process characterization builds the frame for Process Performance Qualification (PPQ), from protocol to execution.TBC #10

  6. 06

    Regulatory support and market entry

    Comparability assessment, scientific advice, dossier support and pre-approval inspection (PAI) support from our internal regulatory team. Drug product through qualified partners1.

Biosimilar vs NBE

How does a biosimilar CDMO differ from an NBE-focused one?

Both run the same core development and GMP manufacturing. The difference is what the evidence has to prove. An NBE program proves a new molecule is safe and effective. A biosimilar program proves similarity to a reference product, mostly through analytics.

Can a European CDMO support a biosimilar program from cell line development through to PPQ?

Yes. Rezon Bio covers cell line development, process and analytical development, scale-up, clinical and commercial drug substance manufacturing and PPQ, with regulatory support through to market authorization, at two FDA- and EMA-approved sites in Poland.TBC #7

How do biosimilar CDMOs differ from NBE-focused manufacturers?

A biosimilar CDMO has to prove similarity to a reference product, so analytics carry most of the evidence and processes are tuned to the reference product’s quality profile. An NBE-focused manufacturer builds evidence for a new molecule’s safety and efficacy. Rezon Bio runs both.

What is the difference between analytical biosimilarity and analytical comparability?

Biosimilarity compares a proposed biosimilar with the licensed reference product. Comparability compares the same product before and after a process change. Both often run in the same biosimilar program, and they answer different questions.

Can analytical data replace a Phase III comparative efficacy study?

In some cases. FDA draft guidance (October 2025) proposes that analytical and pharmacokinetic similarity data may replace a comparative efficacy study in appropriate cases, and the EMA’s March 2026 reflection paper allows a waiver when specific criteria are met. In one recent mAb biosimilar program, the FDA agreed Rezon Bio’s analytical package could support waiving the Phase III trial.TBC #6, #17

Does Rezon Bio have commercially approved biosimilars?

Yes. Rezon Bio’s teams have developed three biosimilars approved globally, two already commercialized through partners. In February 2026, Warsaw-Duchnice received FDA approval for commercial biosimilar manufacturing.

How long does biosimilar development take?

Industry estimates put mAb biosimilar development at six to eight years, with Phase III efficacy studies alone taking one to three. Rezon Bio’s integrated model targets market entry in less than 3 years, depending on the molecule and whether an efficacy study is needed.TBC #5

What analytical data does Rezon Bio generate for biosimilars?

Peptide mapping by LC-MS, chromatography and electrophoresis methods, cell-based and binding potency assays, comparability testing, forced degradation and stability studies, from 78 techniques established in GMP and backed by internal statistical capabilities.

Does Rezon Bio have a PPQ track record for biosimilars?

Yes. Process characterization builds the frame for Process Performance Qualification (PPQ), part of our technology transfer, MSAT and PPQ service.TBC #10

At what scale can Rezon Bio manufacture biosimilars?

Gdańsk runs 50 L, 250 L and 1,000 L single-use bioreactors for clinical and mid-scale supply. Warsaw-Duchnice runs 500 L and 2,000 L for commercial supply, up to 2 x 2,000 L batch scale.

What types of biosimilars does Rezon Bio work on?

Mammalian-expressed biologics, including monoclonal antibodies such as IgG1 mAbs and mAb fragments.TBC #9
